# Sheets

A project can have **several sheets**, like a set of drawings: production on one, safety on another, the GEMMA on another. Together they make up the same automated system.

## Handling

- The tabs are below the canvas. **+** adds a sheet; **double-clicking** the tab renames it.
- To move elements to another sheet: select them and right-click > “Move to …”.
- A link between sheets is drawn as **references**: an arrow “to step 5 (Sheet 2)” at the origin and “from …” at the destination. It is still the same link.

## What sees the whole project

Simulation, the ladder and Verify work with **all sheets** at once. When exporting to PDF you can choose “All sheets”.

> **Watch out:** step numbers must be unique in the whole project, not just on each sheet.
